# YTHR REVERB Rev0 — design report

Reverb pedal board (MCP23017 at 0x25), generated by [gen_reverb.py](gen_reverb.py). Reworked on 2026-11-21 with
KiCad 10.0.2 and the pcb skill, as Effect ([../effect/REPORT.md](../effect/REPORT.md)): same template, bus block,
buttons, encoders and layout; four encoders, the MCP23017 in the square's centre. Decisions behind the shared blocks:
[../README.md](../README.md).

## What changed against rev A

| | rev A (50 × 65) | Rev0 |
|---|---|---|
| Outline | 50 × 65 mm rectangle | enclosure template **`Type_Z-76_Z-77`**, 50 × 104 mm (copy in `common/templates/`), kept as it is; its rim printed along the edge on both sides, **0.3 mm**; nothing beyond the rim but the RJ45 |
| Mask | blue | blue (white silkscreen) |
| Layout | 28 mm square, MCP in the centre, LEDs at the bottom edge, footswitches on wires in the corners | side lines x = 9 / 41 (32 mm apart); encoders in a **32 mm square** (DECAY, PR_DL at y = 21, TONE, MIX at y = 53), the MCP23017 in its centre (25, 38); indicators SPRING / ROOM / PLATE / HALL in a row at y = 65, x = 10 … 40; **Q27 buttons** MODE / ON on the side lines at (9, 87) / (41, 87) |
| Footswitches | wire pads in the bottom corners | **Dailywell Q27 (7MS7)** push buttons soldered into the board, 1 COMM = GND, 2 NO = the wire pad's net (before the debounce), 3 NC open; the wire pads stay between the buttons for external switches in parallel; MODE / ON under the buttons, 1.5 mm |
| Footswitch inputs | wire straight to the GPIO | **hardware debounce**: 10k pull-up, 1k series, 100n at the pin |
| Bus | unshielded RJ45 straight to the MCP | **shielded RJ45 with LEDs** (Ckmtw R-RJ45R08P-C004 footprint; the user's ZSDZFYLLK jack, hand-soldered, not in the BOM): yellow = INT, green = power, 1k each; **PCA9511A hot-swap buffer** (card side SCL_L / SDA_L, 10k pull-ups); **ferrite** on VCC |
| Encoders | PEC11R-4015F-S0024 (with push switch), EasyEDA land pattern | **PEC11R-4015F-N0024, no push switch** (the project needs none; it lets the side encoders sit 9 mm from the edge), Bourns datasheet land pattern and 3D model (shared `PedalLib`) |
| Ground | local label `/GND` | global net **`GND`** |
| Test points | none | 6 pads on the bottom: GND, 3V3, SCL, SDA, INT, RST |
| Markings | "REVERB" on top, "YTHR Reverb rev A" on the back | `YTHR REVERB`, `Rev0`, `Made on the Island` on the back (name / revision from one place in the generator) |
| Routing | the project's own router | **Freerouting** (`tools/freeroute.py`, cached in `routing.pkl`), tracks 0.3 mm from the edge and inside the rim |
| Design rules | KiCad defaults raised by hand | JLCPCB limits and the skill's layout rules (`reverb.kicad_dru`) |

Pin assignment unchanged (feather/doc/wiring.md, Reverb - 0x25): encoders DECAY GPA1/GPB1, PR_DL GPA2/GPB2,
TONE GPA3/GPB3, MIX GPA4/GPB4; indicators SP GPB5, RM GPA5, PT GPB6, HL GPA6; MODE GPB0, ON GPA0.

## Possible problems, assumptions, not verified

- **Encoder requirement:** the project needs 20 pulses and a knurled "plum handle" shaft (README.md); the
  PEC11R-4015F-N0024 here (24 pulses, D-shaft) does not meet it yet — to be replaced when a part is chosen, by one
  without the push switch (with the switch pins a 32 mm square does not fit: they land on the rim line).
- **PEC11R-4015F-N0024 stock:** 77 at JLCPCB (2026-11-21); Gate + Comp, Effect and Reverb together need 65 for
  5 boards each, more than JLCPCB holds now. No alternate with this land pattern was checked.
- **FB1's reference** found no free spot on the silkscreen (the jack area is full): it is on the Fab layer only.
- **Encoders do not fasten to the lid**, the knobs' side load goes into the board, which hangs on the two buttons.
- **RJ45 and Q27 hand-soldered**: compare the user's jack with the C386759 drawing and check the Q27 pins (1 COMM,
  2 NO, 3 NC) with a meter before soldering.
- **Template holes** (4 × Ø2.4 mm): if used, standoffs of 10.43 mm (= Q27 housing).
- **Indicators:** on-board LEDs need their solder jumper closed and a window in the lid (3 mm marks on the drill
  template); the lamp wire pads stay.

## Operating conditions

Same as Effect (same bus block and ICs): 3.3 V from the bus (2.7 … 5.5 V), −40 … +70 °C (PEC11R) … +85 °C;
idle ≈ 4 mA plus 2 × 1.2 mA for the jack's LEDs; worst case (4 on-board LEDs, both buttons held, all encoder
contacts closed) ≈ 24 mA.

## Circuit notes

- **Hot-swap buffer (PCA9511A, NXP datasheet Rev 5.0).** IN side = cable (TVS, optional bus pull-ups behind JP1),
  OUT side = MCP23017 with 10k pull-ups to +3V3 (datasheet application). ENABLE tied to VCC; READY not used (open
  drain, left open). On plugging in, SDA/SCL are precharged to 1 V and the sides connect only after a STOP or an idle
  bus, so plugging a pedal into a running bus does not corrupt a transfer. Card-side rise time with 10k and ~20 pF
  ≈ 170 ns (< 300 ns at 400 kHz); the accelerators require bus pull-up voltage = VCC (both 3.3 V from Main).
  No in-stock drop-in in TSSOP-8; pin-compatible LTC4300A-1 / TCA4307 are MSOP-8 (footprint change).
- **Footswitch debounce** (from the parts, checked in ngspice with the MCP weak pull-up 43k–125k): press — the pin
  falls below VIL (0.2 VDD) in ~0.17 ms, low level 0.03–0.07 V; release — above VIH (0.8 VDD) after ~1.6 ms;
  0.3 ms bounce gaps are filtered. The firmware debounce stays as it is.
- **Encoder filter** unchanged (user's decision B: the prototype values work): 10k / 10k / 10n, edges 0.19 ms falling,
  0.30 ms rising.
- **INT** is not buffered (the PCA9511A carries SDA/SCL only): it goes from the jack's TVS straight to INTA/INTB.
  As before, every MCP must use `IOCON.ODR=1` before the firmware uses INT.

## Possible problems, assumptions, not verified

- **Panel layout is an assumption:** encoders on a 41 mm square (x = 9.5 / 50.5, y = 18 / 59), LEDs at y = 76,
  Q27 buttons at y = 96, in the 1590N1 template. The outer encoders sit 9.5 mm from the board edge: check that the
  knobs clear the enclosure's lid edge and corner screws. Check the drill template against the lid before drilling.
- **Q27 contact numbering:** 1 COMM (end lug) / 2 NO (middle) / 3 NC, as on the maker's drawing (switch function
  "MOM 1-2"); worth one check with a meter before soldering: with COMM in the middle the input would read pressed
  all the time.
- **Encoders do not fasten to the lid** (bushing 0.9 mm short of the outer face): the board hangs on the two buttons
  only, the knobs' side load goes into the board. 7.5 mm holes, not 7 mm (the bushing is inside the hole).
- **Template holes** (4 × Ø2 mm, Edge.Cuts cut-outs): kept as in the template. If they are used, the standoffs must
  be 10.43 mm (= Q27 housing) so the board is not bent.
- **RJ45 LED colours** (yellow left, green right) are the user's jack's; the C386759 datasheet footprint is used for
  it — compare the user's jack with the drawing before soldering.
- **Other boards:** the PEC11R footprint fix in `PedalLib` makes effect, echo, reverb and main fail the library check
  until each is regenerated (separate tasks).
- **Indicators:** no panel lamps (user's decision); the on-board LEDs need their solder jumper closed and a window or
  light pipe in the lid (optional 3 mm marks on the drill template).
- **Encoders are THT**, in the BOM (JLCPCB places them); the RJ45 and the Q27 buttons are hand-soldered.
- **Template silkscreen:** its rim is printed (0.3 mm instead of the template's 0.1 mm); the
  centre lines and the arrow stay on `Dwgs.User` only, as they would cross pads and labels.
- **Stitching vias** may sit under the bottom-side markings; legibility should be checked on the render.
- **Sources:** octopart.com refused automated access (CAPTCHA): lifecycle checked on findchips.com (distributor stock,
  no EOL / NRND flags; MCP23017 projected EOL 2036-06-24) and the JLCPCB library only.
- **PCA9511A alternates** need a different footprint (MSOP-8); the RJ45 has no checked alternate with the same land
  pattern.

### Protection left out (and why)

- 9 V input chain (reverse, over-/under-voltage, PTC, UVLO): not applicable — the board is powered with 3.3 V over the
  bus cable from Main (Feather regulator); the keyed 8P8C jack makes reverse polarity impossible.
- I²C series resistors at the jack (option C): not fitted, user's decision — the buffer isolates the MCP from the
  cable.
- I²C pull-ups: on Main only (bus side), as before; JP1 keeps the optional 10k pair, open.

## Operating temperature

| Part | P (worst case) | θJA (approx.) | Tj at +50 °C inside |
|---|---|---|---|
| PCA9511A | 3.3 V × 6 mA = 20 mW | ~200 K/W (TSSOP-8) | ~54 °C (max 125 °C) |
| MCP23017 | 3.3 V × 1 mA + LED 2 × 3 mA × 1.4 V ≈ 12 mW | ~90 K/W (SSOP-28) | ~51 °C (max 150 °C) |

No hot parts, no heatsink, no cooling. Maximum ambient: +85 °C (PCA9511A, MCP23017-E: −40…+125 °C). Minimum:
−40 °C (PCA9511A); the encoders (PEC11R: −40…+85 °C) and the 10 µF X5R (−55…+85 °C) set the same window. Not
simulated over temperature: there is no analog stage whose behaviour depends on it.

## Operating voltage

3.3 V from the bus. Lowest: 2.7 V (PCA9511A minimum; MCP23017 works from 1.8 V). Highest: 5.5 V (both ICs); the
SRV05-4 clamps transients above ~6 V. The ferrite drops < 10 mV.
